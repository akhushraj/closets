## Must have
- **Hanging:** one level, generous. Jackets hang on the rod too.
- **Folded storage:** shelves and drawers. The closet holds all his clothes (there's no dresser).
- **Hamper:** free-standing on the floor.
- **Long-term storage:** saved Lego sets and keepsakes in the upper zone (above the door head).
- **Lighting:** LED strips inside, switched on automatically (motion or door).
- **Look:** "fancy." Rohan's request.
- **Small items:** an IKEA box on a shelf, not a built-in tray.

## Left out
- Shoes (they go in the laundry-room closet)
- Fencing bag and blades
- Hooks (jackets hang on the rod)

## How it gets built
Custom-cut plywood (preferred) or prefab 3'-wide MDF modules with modular drawers and
shelves. Leaning plywood: the space is irregular and 10' tall.
